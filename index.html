How I work

From brief to live site

Roughly the steps every project goes through on my desk.

  1. 01

    Understand the brief

    Sit with the SEO, ads and content team to know who the site is for and what it has to do.

  2. 02

    Design & build

    Mobile-first layout in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Tailwind or Bootstrap if it speeds things up.

  3. 03

    Test everywhere

    Chrome, Firefox, Safari, phones and tablets. Forms, speed and on-page SEO get checked too.

  4. 04

    Go live & maintain

    Domain, hosting and deployment on cPanel or hPanel, then updates once the site is out.
